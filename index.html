Selected cases

Six engagements. Six different operating levers.

Commercial, operational, contractual and data-led interventions. Full role and delivery detail is available in the case library below.

Client D · Specialized care

Contingent workforce

€3,423,467
Challenge

The organization used approximately 12 to 15 suppliers across a broad range of roles and rates. The hiring process lacked transparency and shifts were regularly left unfilled, incorrectly filled or duplicated.

Intervention

Rates and contractual terms were tightened and standardized. The full workforce process was redesigned, implemented and embedded, including legal provisions on liability, tax treatment and false self-employment risk.

Result

Fill rate 70% → 88%; sharper rates, increased contract coverage and improved compliance.

Client B · Mental health care

Contingent workforce – Care

€753,503
Challenge

The contingent workforce was fragmented across many suppliers. Rates and shift allowances varied, contract coverage was often missing and the requisition process lacked structure.

Intervention

A new flexible workforce model and structured requisition process were implemented. Shifts are offered internally first and then released externally according to a defined supplier sequence. Rates and contractual conditions were standardized and formalized.

Result

Standardized rates, increased contract coverage and a transparent, automated requisition process.

Client F · Elderly care

Food

€362,644
Challenge

The food assortment was very broad and largely unstandardized. Locations purchased different product types and quality levels, limiting scale benefits and control.

Intervention

A core assortment was developed with users, implemented, monitored and refined. The number of products was reduced from approximately 4,600 to 1,000 and the concentrated purchasing base was used in supplier negotiations.

Result

Assortment approximately 4,600 → 1,000 items. Savings mix: approximately 30% pack-size changes, 40% A-to-B brand substitution and 30% price.

Client F · Elderly care

Rent and building maintenance – process optimization

€244,231
Challenge

The maintenance supplier required many repeat visits to resolve issues, creating additional costs and lost time for employees supporting supplier visits.

Intervention

A new request process was designed so users provide more complete information on what is required at the location. Waste was removed with the supplier and preparation for site visits was improved.

Result

First Time Right 44.5% → 78%, exceeding the 75% target.

Client C · Disability care

Office supplies

€147,130
Challenge

Purchasing was fragmented across many small suppliers. This limited volume leverage and created a high volume of low-value invoices for Finance.

Intervention

Tail suppliers were consolidated into one provider. Prices were reduced and ordering, quality and assortment were centralized and standardized.

Result

Suppliers 65 → 1; invoices approximately 14,000 → 12 per year.

Client C · Disability care

Transport claims

€239,338
Challenge

Differences arose between clients actually transported, claims submitted and journeys invoiced, both in quantities and claim types.

Intervention

A standardized control and reconciliation process was designed and implemented to identify and correct differences between journeys, claims and invoicing in a timely manner.

Result

Approximately 700–800 annual mismatches identified at baseline; recurring mismatch detection and follow-up established.
